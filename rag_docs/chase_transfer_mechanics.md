# Chase Ultimate Rewards — Transfer Mechanics

## Transfer mechanics
Eligible Ultimate Rewards points can be transferred to participating frequent-travel programs.

## Important operational rules
- Transfers are made in 1,000-point increments under the published program agreement.
- The receiving loyalty account must satisfy Chase eligibility requirements.
- Transfer processing time can vary by partner.
- Many requests process by the next business day, while some can take longer.
- Verify award availability and the current partner ratio before initiating a transfer.

## Modeling rule
The transfer ratio belongs in the structured transfer-rules table. This RAG document covers process restrictions and cautions.
