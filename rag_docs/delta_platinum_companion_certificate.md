# Delta Platinum Amex — Companion Certificate

## Benefit summary
Eligible Delta SkyMiles Platinum Card Members receive a Companion Certificate after card renewal.

## Key conditions
- Applies to eligible Delta Main round-trip travel.
- Current published coverage includes eligible travel within the U.S. and to Mexico, the Caribbean, or Central America.
- Government-imposed taxes and fees still apply.
- Delta Basic experiences are not eligible.
- Availability and fare-class restrictions can apply.

## How RewardPilot should use this
Treat a Companion Certificate as a separate wallet asset, not as points. If the trip qualifies and the user has an unused certificate, consider it as another booking strategy.
