# Amex Membership Rewards — Delta SkyMiles Transfer Policy

## Transfer relationship

Eligible American Express Membership Rewards points can be transferred to Delta SkyMiles.

The current published transfer ratio is:

- 1,000 Membership Rewards points = 1,000 Delta SkyMiles

## Important conditions

- The Membership Rewards account must be linked to an eligible participating frequent-flyer account.
- The receiving partner account must belong to the Card Member or an eligible Additional Card Member under American Express rules.
- Award availability and the number of miles required should be checked before transferring points.
- Transfers to a partner loyalty program are final.
- After transfer, the points are governed by the partner program's terms.

## U.S. airline transfer fee

For transfers of Membership Rewards points to a U.S. airline frequent-flyer program, American Express currently charges an excise-tax offset fee.

Current published fee:
- $0.0006 per point transferred
- Maximum fee: $99

American Express may allow Membership Rewards points to be used to cover this fee.
