# Amex Platinum — 5X Air and Hotel Eligibility

## Eligible airfare
The elevated airfare earning applies to eligible scheduled passenger flights booked directly with airlines or through American Express Travel. A calendar-year spending cap applies to the elevated airfare rate.

## Eligible hotels
The elevated hotel earning applies to eligible prepaid hotel reservations booked through American Express Travel.

## Exclusions
Published terms exclude some categories such as charter/private-jet travel, some packaged travel, ticketing/service fees, cancellation/change fees, and certain property/group-event charges.

## How RewardPilot should use this
Do not apply the Platinum travel multiplier merely because the purchase is travel. Check the merchant, booking channel, and transaction type.
