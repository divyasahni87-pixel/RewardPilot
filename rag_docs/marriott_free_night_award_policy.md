# Marriott Bonvoy — Free Night Award Rules

## Benefit concept
A Free Night Award is a certificate-like reward that can cover one eligible night up to the certificate's stated points level, subject to Marriott rules and hotel availability.

## Important conditions
- The specific certificate value depends on the award issued to the member.
- Incidental charges and mandatory resort fees can remain payable.
- A Free Night Award cannot simply be converted to cash.
- Certain Free Night Awards can be combined with additional Bonvoy points within stated limits.
- Free Night Awards have expiration rules.

## How RewardPilot should use this
Store each user's actual Free Night Award as a wallet asset with value and expiration date. RAG provides the policy; the wallet provides whether the certificate exists.
