# American Express Gold — Travel Earning Conditions

## Flights
The Gold Card earns an elevated Membership Rewards rate on eligible flights booked directly with airlines or through American Express Travel.

## Prepaid hotels
The Gold Card earns an elevated Membership Rewards rate on eligible prepaid hotel bookings through AmexTravel.com or the Amex Travel App.

## Important distinction
Booking channel matters. A hotel booked directly with a hotel is not the same earning category as an eligible prepaid hotel booked through American Express Travel.

## How RewardPilot should use this
The agent must classify both purchase category and booking channel before applying the structured earn multiplier from the card database.
