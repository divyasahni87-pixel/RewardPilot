# Delta SkyMiles Cards — TakeOff 15

## What the benefit does
Eligible Basic Card Members can receive at least a 15% discount on the mileage portion of eligible Delta Award Tickets.

## Key eligibility conditions
- The itinerary must be entirely on Delta- or Delta Connection-operated and ticketed flights.
- The booking must be made through delta.com or the Fly Delta app.
- The eligible card must be linked to the SkyMiles account used for booking.
- The user must have enough SkyMiles for the full discounted award ticket.

## Important exclusions
- Partner-operated flights are not eligible.
- Taxes and fees are not discounted.
- The benefit does not apply to Pay with Miles, Miles + Cash, or post-booking seat upgrades.
- Award availability is required.
- The benefit generally cannot be stacked with certificates, eCredits, gift cards, vouchers, or other discounts.

## How RewardPilot should use this
