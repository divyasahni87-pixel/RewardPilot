# Eligible Delta Amex Cards — Checked Bag Benefits

## First checked bag
The Basic Card Member can receive a first standard checked bag fee waiver on eligible Delta itineraries.

## Key conditions
- The reservation must include the Basic Card Member's SkyMiles number.
- The benefit can extend to passengers on the same reservation, subject to program limits.
- It applies only to a standard checked bag that is not overweight or oversized.
- Eligible segments must be Delta-marketed and Delta-operated or Delta Connection-operated, with check-in through Delta.
- Codeshare flights are not eligible.

## Second checked bag
Current published terms also describe a second checked bag waiver on qualifying domestic itineraries for eligible Delta Amex cards.

## How RewardPilot should use this
When comparing cash-booking cards for a Delta flight, the agent can consider baggage savings as a non-points benefit, but only when itinerary and traveler conditions qualify.
